VIBEOS Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VIBEOS (“VIBEOS,” “we,” “our,” or “the service”) may collect, use, store, and share information when you use its Instagram account connection, content-management, scheduling, and AI-assisted features.

1. Information we may process

  • Account and connection information: Instagram account identifiers, profile information made available through Meta’s APIs, authorization tokens, and connection status.
  • Content you choose to use: photos, videos, captions, hashtags, scheduled-post details, and other content you submit or ask VIBEOS to manage.
  • Engagement information: comments and related identifiers or timestamps needed for comment-management and reply features that you enable.
  • Service and diagnostic information: settings, feature choices, error details, and operational logs needed to run, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

The information available to VIBEOS depends on the permissions you grant, the features you use, and the access permitted by Meta.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to connect your account, provide requested content-management and scheduling functions, generate captions or suggested replies, carry out actions you enable, maintain security, diagnose errors, and comply with legal requirements. Automated actions should only be enabled when you intend VIBEOS to perform them; review your settings and generated content.

3. AI and service providers

When you use AI-assisted features, relevant text or content may be sent to the AI provider configured for that feature to generate a result. VIBEOS may also rely on hosting, database, and infrastructure providers to operate the service. Those providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their applicable terms and policies. Check the applicable provider policies before enabling a feature that sends content to that provider.

4. Meta and Instagram

Instagram account access is provided through Meta’s authorization and APIs. Your use of Instagram remains subject to Meta’s and Instagram’s applicable terms and policies. VIBEOS uses API information to provide the features you request and does not claim ownership of your Instagram content.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers described above, disclosed when required by law, or used to protect the service, its users, and others. We do not intend to use Instagram API data for unrelated purposes.

6. Storage, security, and retention

Information may be stored in the service’s configured database, hosting environment, or other systems used to operate VIBEOS. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are intended to protect information, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain necessary records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations, and may remain in backups or logs for a limited period.

7. Your choices and deletion requests

You can stop using VIBEOS and revoke its access through the relevant Meta or Instagram settings. To request deletion of information associated with your VIBEOS connection, contact the project maintainer through the VIBEOS-Backend GitHub repository. Include enough information to identify the account or request, but do not post passwords, access tokens, or other secrets publicly. We may need to verify the request before acting. Some information may be retained where legally required or necessary for security and legitimate operational purposes.

8. Children

VIBEOS is not intended for children who are not permitted to use the service under applicable law or the relevant platform terms. Do not use the service if you are not eligible to do so.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as VIBEOS changes. The effective date at the top indicates when this version was published. Continued use after an update is subject to the revised policy to the extent permitted by law.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the project maintainer through the VIBEOS-Backend GitHub repository.

Important: This policy is a project draft and should be checked against the actual production configuration, data-retention practices, AI providers, and contact process before submitting it to Meta.
